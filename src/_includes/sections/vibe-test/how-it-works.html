03 — How it works

A closed loop. Not a checklist.

Closed loop
01 Discover 02 Fix 03 Verify 04 Ship
  1. 01
    Discover

    Agents map your app and surface what's broken.

  2. 02
    Fix

    They author the fix and open a reviewable PR.

  3. 03
    Verify

    Re-tested and machine-checked into a receipt.

  4. 04
    Ship

    Merged, deployed, and looped back for the next.
